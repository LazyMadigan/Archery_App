# Privacy

**Short version: your archery data never leaves your phone.**

- Archery App has no accounts, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. Nobody involved is paid.
- Everything you enter (profile, photo, club colours, rounds, arrows, courses, sight marks, traced rings) is stored only in your phone's browser storage (`localStorage`). It is never sent to us or anyone else.

## What does go online
Three things make an ordinary web request, like any website you visit. They can see the request itself (for example your IP address and browser), but none of your archery data is in it.
- **GitHub Pages** serves the app's files when you open it.
- **Google Fonts** serves the display lettering.
- **archery.ie**: the news slide on the home screen asks archery.ie for its latest headlines (at most every three hours, and only when you're online). If the site doesn't allow that, the slide just links to it.

## GDPR
We don't collect, receive or store any personal data, so there is nothing held about you anywhere else to request, correct or delete. You control your data on the phone:
- **Profile → Export my data** gives you a full copy as an Excel file (your photo is left out).
- **Profile → Delete all my data** erases everything the app stores on the phone, after two confirmations.
- Clearing your browser's site data for the app does the same. There is no copy anywhere else, so export first if you want one.

## Sharing is always your choice
"Save image" / "Share" on a summary or sight chart, and Excel exports, make files that go only where you send them. "Copy JSON" in the tracing tool puts text on your clipboard; it only reaches us if you paste it somewhere.

## Contact
Questions or something not right: open an issue at https://github.com/LazyMadigan/Archery_App/issues (Profile → Privacy & contact → Contact us). The source code is all public, so anyone can check this is true.

This is a plain description of how the app works, not legal advice.
