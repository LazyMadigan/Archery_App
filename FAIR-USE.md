# Fair use

This is a free, volunteer-built tool for archers. We want it used.

**You can**
- Use it for your own shooting, your club, your friends, your have-a-go day, your competition — no permission needed, no fee, ever.
- Put the link on your club's website or WhatsApp group.
- Fork it, rebrand it for your club, change the scoring tables, add your own targets. The code is MIT licensed (see LICENSE).
- Send improvements back — traced rings, new targets, bug fixes, better words. Credit goes in the README.

**Please don't**
- Charge people for access to this app or a lightly modified copy of it. Charging for your own event or coaching that happens to use it is of course fine.
- Add tracking, ads or data collection to a copy and still call it Archery App. Rename it so people know it's different.
- Present it as an official scoring system of IFAA, World Archery, NFAS, ASA, IBO or any federation. It isn't. The points tables are our best understanding of how those rounds are commonly scored and are editable precisely because clubs differ. For a record score, use the federation's own scorecard.

**Target photos**
The library currently uses manufacturers' product photos (SRT, Eleven Targets, Rinehart, Delta McKenzie) — converted to greyscale, used only to identify the animal and show where its scoring rings are, in a non-commercial club tool. We're replacing them with our own photos of our own targets over time, and if any maker would rather theirs weren't here, open an issue and they'll be removed the same day.

**No warranty**
It's a scoring aid written in spare time. If it loses your scores, misreads a ring, or disagrees with the marshal — the marshal wins.
