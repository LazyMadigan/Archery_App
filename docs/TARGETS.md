# Target library notes

How each 3D target was identified, where its picture comes from, and the state of its scoring zones.

## Method
1. The archer photographed each target on the Dargle Valley course (red pegs, 15 Nov 2026).
2. Each animal was matched to a manufacturer model by comparing the course photos with retailer product shots (Merlin Archery, JVD Archery, Leitold, Fairbow, Wales Archery). The archer identified targets 14, 20 and 22.
3. Product photos are converted to **greyscale cut-outs** (background removed, outline added) for the in-app diagram. The bedded fox's rear view came from a photo the archer supplied.
4. Scoring zones are **traced from the target's moulded ring lines** and stored as polygons in percent of the photo. Untraced faces are scored with the zone buttons.

## Known gaps
- **Rings:** only the fox is traced. The plan is a desktop tracer with zoom and snap-to-groove.
- **Other sides:** most targets have only one side in the library. The archer will photograph all sides.
- **Photos:** manufacturer product shots for identification only; to be replaced with the club's own photos. Any maker who'd rather theirs weren't used can open an issue and it will be removed the same day (see FAIR-USE.md).
- **Polar bear:** cut-out was re-done after the original lost part of its rump (white bear on white background).
