# 3D Fun Round — how to use it

Open the link on your phone. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone; the "Install" prompt or ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android). After that it opens like an app and works with no signal.

## Your profile
The first time you open the app it asks you to set up your profile (you can skip). Everything you enter is stored only on your phone: there's no account and no server, so the app's developer can't see it.

Tap the round picture top-right on the home screen, or open **Settings → your profile card**. Make your changes and press **Save profile**; if you leave without saving, it asks before discarding. Add your name, bow (recurve, compound, barebow, traditional), Archery Ireland number, club and club number, and a photo if you like. Your name and bow fill in Player 1 automatically on every round. Your **club** name shows at the top of the home screen, and if you paste your club's **booking link** (e.g. a Google Form), the **Booking** tile on the home screen opens it. It all stays on your phone.

Your profile also shows your **track record**: rounds and arrows shot, and **personal bests** for every round you've shot (kept separately per round, distance, face and bow).

## Export my data (Excel)
At the bottom of your profile, **Export to Excel** downloads one file with everything on this phone: your profile (no photo), every round, every indoor/field arrow (with where it hit, if you tapped the face), every 3D target you shot (animal, side, peg, distance, slope, zone, points, sight setting, distance guess), your courses and your sight marks. **Share…** sends it straight to another app.

The first sheet, **Read me**, explains every column and the scoring rules, and includes a suggested prompt. That way you can give the file to an AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) and ask whether you're improving, where your arrows go, which distances cost you points, and what to practise. The file is built on your phone; nothing is uploaded unless you share it.

## Appearance
Settings → **Appearance**: System (follows your phone), Light or Dark.

## Three disciplines
The home screen has **3D Course**, **Indoor** and **Field**.

### Indoor
Pick the archers and each one's bow, then the round: **WA 18** (60 arrows), **Half WA 18**, **Vegas 300** (3-spot), **Portsmouth**, or **Custom**. Choose the distance (**18 / 15 / 10 m**), the face (**40 / 60 / 80 cm** or **3-spot**), the number of ends and arrows per end.

**Compound scoring:** on the 40 cm and 3-spot faces, compound only scores 10 in the small inner ring (the X10); the rest of the 10 ring counts 9. Recurve, barebow and traditional get the full 10. The app applies this automatically from each archer's bow.

### Field
Pick **WA Field** (6–1: yellow 6–5, black 4–1) or **IFAA Field** (5-4-3), the distance (**10–70 m** in tens), the face (**20 / 40 / 60 / 80 cm**), and the number of targets. Scoring is the same for every bow; barebow normally shoots from the blue peg, recurve and compound from red.

### Scoring indoor and field
**Tap the face where each arrow landed**: it works out the score and plots the arrow. Or tap the score buttons. Only tapped arrows appear on your group plot. Tap an arrow chip to clear it. **Next end** (or **Next target** on field) when everyone's shot.

### Games (indoor and field)
- **Total points** — highest total wins.
- **King of the Castle** — the best end takes the crown; hold it to stay King.
- **Set play** — the Olympic match format: 2 set points for winning an end, 1 each for a tie. Head to head, first to 6 wins.

The summary shows totals, X's, 10s, average per arrow, and each archer's arrow group on the face.

## Shoot a 3D round
1. **Who's shooting** — type names (they're remembered next time). Up to six. Tick **Handicap** if you want points-per-target added for weaker archers; it suggests a number from past rounds and you can change it.
2. **Scoring** — pick the system your group uses. Arrows per target is capped at 3 and by the system (World Archery 3D is 2, IFAA Hunting is 1, NFAS is walk-down up to 3). You can edit the points for any system in Settings.
3. **What are we doing** — *Fun round* (score against each other) or *Laser practice* (see below).
   **Games** (fun round) — tick any mix:
   - **Total points** — the usual.
   - **King of the Castle** — best single arrow on the target wins it. Win and you're King; the King keeps the crown on a tie. Scoreboard shows holes won and the current streak.
   - **Skins** — each target is worth one skin; if nobody wins outright, it carries to the next (two skins riding, three…).
   **Add-on: Guess the distance** — layers over any game. At each target everyone calls the distance *first*; scoring stays locked until the calls are locked in. Lase it after shooting. 5 pts within 1 m, 3 within 2 m, 1 within 3 m, on its own leaderboard.
4. **Course** — load a saved course, build one now (category → animal → side, then drag to order), or leave it as *Pick as we go*.
5. **Start shooting.**

## At the peg
- If you didn't pre-build the course, you get the target picker: tap the type of animal, the animal, and (if it has rings on more than one side) which side you're facing. Three taps, or use the search box.
- The picture shows the actual target in greyscale with the scoring rings coloured: **yellow** centre, **red** inner, **blue** vital; anything else on the animal is **body**. The legend shows the points for your system.
- Score an archer by **tapping the picture where the arrow landed** (the app reads the zone from the rings) or by tapping a **zone button**. Tap a player's row to switch who you're scoring. Tap an arrow chip to clear it.
- **Next target ›** when everyone's in. **Board** any time for standings. **⇄** swaps the animal if you picked wrong.
- Finish from the Board. You get a summary card you can save or share as an image.

## Laser practice
For sighting in, alone or together. At each target: lase the distance, enter the slope if there is one (the app shows the cut distance), enter the sight setting each archer used, shoot, score as normal. Every shot adds a point to your **sight chart** (Progress → Sight chart): distance against setting with a fitted curve and a 5 m lookup table. Once you have a few points, Laser practice suggests a setting for the distance you've just lased.

## Progress
Per archer, for every discipline. Indoor and field: each round type gets its own trend line, last score, PB, and your group over the last five rounds. 3D: average points per target across rounds (per scoring system), best round, where your arrows land (centre / inner / vital / body / miss), distance-judging error, and crowns won. The trend line needs two or more rounds.

## Trace scoring rings — please help with this
Not every target has its rings traced yet. Until it is, you can still score with the buttons, but tapping the picture only records "body".

Tracing takes a minute and helps everyone: **Home → Trace scoring rings**, choose the animal and side, pick the layer (Vital / Inner / Centre), tap around the moulded line on the photo (8–14 taps), **Save to this phone**. Your phone uses it immediately. Then **Copy JSON** and paste it into a GitHub issue (or send it to whoever gave you the link) and it gets added to the shared library for every club.

Targets with rings on two places (e.g. the bedded fox: shoulder and hip) use **+ set** for the second group.

## Your data
Stays on your phone. See PRIVACY.md. Settings → Export backup makes a file you can keep.

## Indoor and Field practice
In Indoor or Field setup, **What are we doing** offers *Fun round* (score against each other, with a game) or *Practice*. Practice asks for **a goal for the session** and won't start until you write one (e.g. "hold at full draw until the shot breaks"). The goal stays at the top of the screen while you shoot and on the scoreboard. When you finish, the summary shows the goal again with a **How did it go?** box for notes. The goal and notes are saved with the round and show in History. Practice scores still count towards progress and personal bests.
