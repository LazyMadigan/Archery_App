# Archery App — how to use it

Open the link on your phone. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone; the "Install" prompt or ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android). After that it opens like an app and works with no signal.

## The home screen
- **Top bar:** Archery App with your club's name underneath, and your profile picture on the right. Tap the picture for your profile and Settings.
- **Top card** (swipe, or tap the three dots): **Shoot well** · **Your best** (your top personal bests, best 3D round and latest achievement; tap it for Progress) · **Archery Ireland news** (latest headlines from archery.ie when the site allows it, otherwise a button that opens archery.ie).
- **Shoot now:** **3D Course**, **Indoor**, **Field**. A round in progress shows as a card here with Continue, Scoreboard, Stop & save and Delete.
- **Off the line:** Progress · History · Booking · Sight marks · 3D Courses · 3D Targets · Achievements.

## Your profile
The first time you open the app it asks you to set up your profile (you can skip). Everything you enter is stored only on your phone: there's no account and no server, so the app's developer can't see it.

Tap your picture on the home screen. Once set up, your details show greyed out under your photo: bows, Archery Ireland number, club, club number and club colours. Tap **Edit details** to change them, then **Save profile** (or **Cancel**). Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.

In **Edit details**:
- **Name** fills Player 1 automatically on every round.
- **Bows I shoot:** tick every bow; rounds start with the bow you shot last.
- **Club:** pick from the list (grouped by province) or choose **Other** and type it. Your club shows at the top of the home screen.
- **Club colours:** tap the tile to open the picker: 12 ready-made pairs, your own main and second colour, or the app default. The whole app previews it straight away; **Done** keeps it, **Save profile** saves it. If a colour would make button text hard to read, the app uses a slightly deeper shade.
- **Club booking link** (e.g. a Google Form): the **Booking** tile on the home screen opens it. It's only shown while editing.
- **Photo:** tap the circle.

Below your details: **Settings**, your **track record** (rounds, arrows, personal bests for every round you've shot, kept separately per round, distance, face and bow), **See progress**, **Achievements**, **Export my data**, and **Privacy & contact**.

## Settings
Profile → **Settings** (button under your details, or the cog top right).
- **Appearance:** System (follows your phone), Light or Dark.
- **3D points per zone:** edit the points for any 3D scoring system if your club scores differently; Reset to default.
- **Clear all:** deletes all rounds and courses (not your profile or sight marks).
- **About, privacy & fair use**, including the ring tracing tool.

## Three disciplines

### Indoor
Pick the archers and each one's bow, then the round: **WA 18** (60 arrows), **Half WA 18**, **Vegas 300** (3-spot), **Portsmouth**, or **Custom**. Choose the distance (**18 / 15 / 10 m**), the face (**40 / 60 / 80 cm** or **3-spot**), the number of ends and arrows per end.

**Compound scoring:** on the 40 cm and 3-spot faces, compound only scores 10 in the small inner ring (the X10); the rest of the 10 ring counts 9. Recurve, barebow and traditional get the full 10. The app applies this automatically from each archer's bow.

### Field
Pick **WA Field** (6–1: yellow 6–5, black 4–1) or **IFAA Field** (5-4-3), the distance (**10–70 m** in tens), the face (**20 / 40 / 60 / 80 cm**), and the number of targets. Scoring is the same for every bow; barebow normally shoots from the blue peg, recurve and compound from red.

### What are we doing (indoor and field)
- **Competition:** score against each other, with a game (below).
- **Practice:** asks for **a goal for the session** and won't start until you write one (e.g. "hold at full draw until the shot breaks"). The goal stays at the top of the screen while you shoot and on the scoreboard. The summary shows it again with a **How did it go?** box. Goal and notes are saved with the round and show in History. Practice scores still count towards progress and personal bests.

### Games (indoor and field competition)
- **Total points:** highest total wins.
- **King of the Castle:** choose how many ends make a crown battle (1, 2, 4 or any number). The best total over the battle takes the crown; if the King ties for best they keep it. Most battles won ranks first; 🔥 shows battles defended in a row.
- **Last archer standing:** three lives each. The lowest end total loses a life (all tied-lowest lose one; if everyone ties, nobody does). Last archer with a life wins.
- **Climb the ladder:** beat your last end to climb a rung, match it to stay, drop below it to slip down one. Highest rung wins. Good on your own.
- **Bingo:** hit each of the top five rings at least once (an X counts as 10; three rings on an IFAA field face). Fewest arrows to fill the card wins.
- **Set play:** the Olympic match format: 2 set points for winning an end, 1 each for a tie. Head to head, first to 6 wins.

Picking King of the Castle or Set play shows a short how-to-play pop-up: **Skip**, **Got it**, or tick **Don't show this again**.

### Scoring indoor and field
**Tap the face where each arrow landed**: it works out the score and plots the arrow. Or tap the score buttons. Only tapped arrows appear on your group plot. Tap an arrow chip to clear it. **Next end** (or **Next target** on field) when everyone's shot. The summary shows totals, X's, 10s, average per arrow, and each archer's arrow group on the face; save or share it as an image.

## Shoot a 3D round
1. **Who's shooting:** type names (they're remembered next time). Up to six. Tick **Handicap** if you want points-per-target added for weaker archers; it suggests a number from past rounds and you can change it.
2. **Scoring:** pick the system your group uses. Arrows per target follow the system (World Archery 3D is 2, IFAA Hunting is 1, NFAS is walk-down up to 3). You can edit the points for any system in Settings.
3. **What are we doing:** *Fun round* (score against each other) or *Laser practice* (see below).
   **Game** (fun round), one at a time:
   - **Total points:** the usual.
   - **King of the Castle:** choose how many targets make a crown battle (1, 2, 4 or any number). Your best arrow on each target counts; the highest battle total takes the crown, and the King keeps it on a tie. Scoreboard shows crowns won and the 🔥 streak.
   - **Last archer standing:** three lives each; the lowest score on a target loses a life; last one standing wins.
   - **Climb the ladder:** beat your last target score to climb a rung; highest rung wins.
   - **3D Bingo:** hit the centre, inner, vital and body at least once each; fewest arrows wins.
   - **Skins:** each target is worth one skin; if nobody wins outright, it carries to the next (two skins riding, three…).
   **Add-on: Guess the distance** layers over any game. At each target everyone calls the distance *first*; scoring stays locked until the calls are locked in. Lase it after shooting. 5 pts within 1 m, 3 within 2 m, 1 within 3 m, on its own leaderboard.
   Each game shows its rules the first time you pick it (skippable).
4. **Course:** load a saved course, build one now (category → animal → side, then drag to order), or leave it as *Pick as we go*. Choose which peg you're shooting from.
5. **Start shooting.**

## At the peg
- If you didn't pre-build the course, you get the target picker: tap the type of animal, the animal, and (if it has rings on more than one side) which side you're facing. Three taps, or use the search box.
- The picture shows the actual target in greyscale with the scoring rings coloured: **yellow** centre, **red** inner, **blue** vital; anything else on the animal is **body**. The legend shows the points for your system.
- Score an archer by **tapping the picture where the arrow landed** (the app reads the zone from the rings) or by tapping a **zone button**. Tap a player's row to switch who you're scoring. Tap an arrow chip to clear it.
- **Next target ›** when everyone's in. **Board** any time for standings. **⇄** swaps the animal if you picked wrong.
- Finish from the Board. You get a summary card you can save or share as an image.

## Laser practice (3D)
For sighting in on a 3D course. At each target: lase the distance, enter the slope if there is one (the app shows the cut distance), enter the sight setting each archer used, shoot, score as normal. Every shot adds a point to your **sight chart** (Progress → Sight chart): distance against setting with a fitted curve and a 5 m lookup table. Once you have a few points, Laser practice suggests a setting for the distance you've just lased.

## Sight marks
Home → **Sight marks** keeps a sight mark chart for each bow, grouped by bow type (all your compounds together). Each tile shows the bow name and the date you sighted it in.

**+ New sight chart:** name the bow (e.g. "30lb Hoyt"), its type and an optional note, tick the distances you have targets at (or add your own), and set arrows per distance. At each distance:
1. Shoot, then tap **↑ High / ● On / ↓ Low** for each arrow. The app tells you which way to move the sight (group high → sight up).
2. **+ Extra arrow** adds another arrow at that distance, e.g. to check after moving the sight. **Reset** clears the calls.
3. Enter the setting your group was on with (it suggests one from the curve once it has a couple of marks). **Next distance**, or **Finish & build chart**.

The chart fits a curve and lists a setting every 5 m across your range; bold rows were shot, the rest come from the curve.
- **Tap any row** to change its setting, delete the mark, or save a curve value as a real mark. The Marks list also has **Edit** and **Delete** buttons, and you can add a mark you already know.
- **Edit** (top right) renames the bow, changes its type or note, or deletes the chart.
- **Save / Share image** (a portrait card with the curve and a big distance → setting table), **Export / Share Excel**, or **Shoot a session** to update it. **Export all charts to Excel** on the list puts every bow in one workbook.

## Progress
Per archer, for every discipline. Indoor and field: each round type gets its own trend line, last score, PB, and your group over the last five rounds. 3D: average points per target across rounds (per scoring system), best round, where your arrows land (centre / inner / vital / body / miss), distance-judging error, crowns won, and the laser-practice sight chart. The trend line needs two or more rounds.

## Achievements
Home → **Achievements** (or Profile). 60 badges earned from rounds where you are an archer (your profile name): arrows shot, rounds, firsts, weekly habits and streaks (weeks run Monday–Sunday), variety, indoor & field scores, personal bests, 3D scores, games won and sight marks. Count-based ones show a progress bar. New ones pop up when you save a round. Deleting a round removes anything that depended on it.

## Export my data (Excel)
Profile → **Export to Excel** downloads one file with everything on this phone: your profile (no photo), every round, every indoor/field arrow (with where it hit, if you tapped the face), every 3D target you shot (animal, side, peg, distance, slope, zone, points, sight setting, distance guess), your courses and your sight marks (laser practice and Sight marks charts). **Share…** sends it straight to another app.

The first sheet, **Read me**, explains every column and the scoring rules, and includes a suggested prompt, so you can give the file to an AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) and ask whether you're improving, where your arrows go, which distances cost you points, and what to practise. The file is built on your phone; nothing is uploaded unless you share it.

## Trace scoring rings — please help with this
Not every target has its rings traced yet. Until it is, you can still score with the buttons, but tapping the picture only records "body".

Tracing takes a minute and helps everyone: Profile → **Settings** → **About, privacy & fair use** → **Open the tracing tool**. Choose the animal and side, pick the layer (Vital / Inner / Centre), tap around the moulded line on the photo (8–14 taps), **Save to this phone**. Your phone uses it immediately. Then **Copy JSON** and paste it into a GitHub issue (or send it to whoever gave you the link) and it gets added to the shared library for every club.

Targets with rings on two places (e.g. the bedded fox: shoulder and hip) use **+ set** for the second group.

## Privacy and your data
Profile → **Privacy & contact** (bottom of the page):
- Everything stays on your phone; nothing is sent to us. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
- **Export my data** gives you a full copy (Excel). There is no other copy anywhere, so export first if you want to keep your history.
- **Delete all my data** erases everything the app stores on this phone (profile, photo, rounds, courses, sight marks, settings) after two confirmations.
- **Contact us** opens the project's issues page.
